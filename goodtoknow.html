Fuel + Water

Fuel & Hydration

It is important to fuel your body before, during and after the race. The Copenhagen Marathon has a number of water stations along the route, where you can get water and energy drinks and banana.

We also recommend that you bring your own energy gels or snacks if you need extra fuel during the race.

Race Tag

Bib & Chip

Your race tag (bib) is your identification during the race. It contains your race number and a timing chip that records your time.

Make sure to wear your bib on the front of your shirt and do not fold or alter it in any way.

Warm Up

Pre-Race Routine

Warming up before the race is important to prepare your muscles and prevent injuries. We recommend a light jog and some dynamic stretches before the start.

Toilets & Help

Medical Aid & Facilities

There are several restroom facilities available along the race route. These are clearly marked and easily accessible.
