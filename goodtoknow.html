Bag Drop & Storage

Do you need to store a little more luggage than your race behind while you run the marathon? Maybe you have some values you are particularly fond of.

Then Volt can help you. Here you can rent a locked cupboard, which is yours on race day.

The cabinets are in Runners Area – close to start and finish – and they are both safe and easy to use.

Dimensions of the cabinet: 30x30x50
